Phanes Arrives & The Sovereigns Fall

The Primordial One (Phanes) arrives in Teyvat and battles the Seven Dragon Sovereigns for forty years. With the creation of the Four Shades, Phanes creates heaven, earth, and the first unified human civilization.

"When the Doves Held Branches: The Primordial One may have been Phanes. It had wings and a crown, and was born from an egg, neither male nor female..."

The Archon War Circa 3,700 – 2,000 YA

The Fall of Decarabian & Rise of Barbatos

In Old Mondstadt (Stormterror's Lair), the nameless bard, Amos, the knight, and a wind spirit lead an uprising against the God of Storms, Decarabian. Decarabian falls, and the wind spirit ascends as the Anemo Archon, Barbatos.

"A nameless wind spirit joined with a young boy... and from the fury of the storm arose the breeze of freedom."
— Biography of Gunnhildr & Breeze Amidst the Forest
Factions: Gunnhildr Clan, Old Mondstadt Location: Mondstadt
The Cataclysm 500 Years Ago

The Fall of Khaenri'ah & Abyssal Outbreak

The subterranean eclipse dynasty of Khaenri'ah collapses under abyssal corruption. Celestia and the Seven Archons intervene, leaving Khaenri'ah ruined and its people afflicted with the curse of the wilderness and immortality.

"The black sun fell, and the disaster brought contamination from the deepest void. Monsters of the Abyss surged across the seven nations..."
— Deepwood Memories & Breeze Amidst the Forest Vol. 2
Factions: Khaenri'ah, Abyss Order, The Seven Location: Khaenri'ah & Global Teyvat
Modern Era Present Day

Hello World: The Traveler Awakens

Separated from their twin by the Sustainer of Heavenly Principles, the Traveler awakens on the shores of Starfell Beach in Mondstadt, fishes up Paimon, and begins their journey across the Seven Nations of Teyvat.

"Two twins, traveling between worlds, were intercepted by an unknown god... So began the journey through the seven kingdoms."
— Archon Quest Prologue: Act I - The Outlander Who Caught the Wind
Factions: Traveler & Paimon Location: Mondstadt (Starfell Beach)
