# 📖 Canonical Lore Glossary & Official Localization Reference

This document serves as the project's **canonical terminology authority** for events, historical eras, characters, places, and cosmological concepts in *Genshin Impact*. All translations, spellings, diacritics, and capitalizations adhere to the official English in-game localization verified against the [Genshin Impact Wiki](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Genshin_Impact_Wiki).
